## Reference direction

The current ElevenLabs marketing homepage was used as a reference for high-level page rhythm and visual language: compact sticky navigation, large editorial hero, product preview, audience strip, platform feature modules, technical/developer content, a dedicated trust/safety section, closing CTA and multi-column footer.

BRɅIN uses its own product language, brand mark, interface treatment and original copy. ElevenLabs' name, logo, customer logos, proprietary marketing copy, illustrations and brand assets are not reproduced. This page is a BRɅIN product page and does not imply an ElevenLabs relationship.

## Trust badge

The highlighted badge says **BRɅIN trust-focused design**. It is a product-design indicator, not a third-party certification or compliance seal.
