Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 November 2026. This policy describes how BRɅIN processes information when organizations and their authorized adult users access the platform.

BRɅIN platforms and voice interfaces are designed exclusively for adult professionals, researchers, and enterprise users aged 18 and older.

Information processed

Depending on the services enabled, BRɅIN may process organization and account details, authorized-user details, uploaded documents, text prompts, retrieved passages, PDF page references, generated responses, usage records, technical diagnostics and support correspondence. Customers are responsible for having the rights and authority to submit the content they provide.

Voice and audio data

The interactive voice preview on this website uses the browser's built-in speech synthesis feature where supported. The preview does not make an ElevenLabs API request. BRɅIN's ElevenLabs Voice AI integration is in development and is not represented as a generally available production feature. Before a production voice feature is enabled, the applicable provider and data flow will be identified in the relevant product notice. Where enabled, audio inputs, text supplied for speech generation and generated audio may be processed by the configured speech provider solely to deliver the requested feature.

Do not upload or submit voice recordings, personal information or other content belonging to anyone under 18.

Workspace separation and access

Customer workspaces are logically separated. Access to uploaded sources, indexed passages, retrieval results and related records is scoped through application identity, project grants and source permissions. The private retrieval service checks authorization before returning evidence. Customer content is not intentionally exposed to another tenant.

How information is used

Information is processed to provide document ingestion, indexing, source-grounded retrieval, citation display, voice features when enabled, service security, quota controls, reliability, support and operational improvements. Infrastructure providers may process data as needed to operate the configured service. BRɅIN does not sell customer documents or personal information.

Retention and deletion

Information is retained for the period required to provide the service, meet agreed operational needs, protect the platform or comply with applicable obligations. An authorized organization representative may request deletion or assistance with a data request by contacting partners@brvin.in. Requests are subject to identity and authority verification, technical feasibility and applicable legal retention requirements.

Security and changes

BRɅIN applies access controls and operational safeguards appropriate to the service. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. This policy may be updated as the platform develops; the effective date above will be revised when material changes are published.

Contact

Privacy questions and authorized data requests may be sent to partners@brvin.in.
