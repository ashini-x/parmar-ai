01 / Data stewardship

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 November 2026. This policy describes how BRɅIN handles information when organizations and their authorized users access its software infrastructure.

Information processed

Depending on the service configuration, BRɅIN may process organization and account details, uploaded documents, learner questions, generated responses, source references, usage records, technical diagnostics, and support correspondence. Organizations are responsible for ensuring that they have the authority to submit the content they provide.

Tenant-level separation

Customer workspaces are logically separated. Access to uploaded sources, indexed passages, retrieval results, and related records is scoped to the tenant and its authorized users. Tenant authorization is enforced as part of the service access model. Customer content is not intentionally exposed to another tenant.

How information is used

Information is processed to provide document ingestion, indexing, retrieval, citation-grounded responses, interactive assessments, service security, quota controls, reliability, and customer support. Infrastructure providers may process data only as needed to operate the configured service. BRɅIN does not sell or resell customer documents, learner questions, or personal data to third parties.

Retention and deletion

Information is retained for the period required to provide the service, meet agreed operational needs, protect the platform, or comply with applicable obligations. An authorized organization representative may request deletion or assistance with a data request by emailing partners@brvin.in. Requests are subject to identity and authority verification, technical feasibility, and any applicable legal retention requirements.

Security and changes

BRɅIN applies access controls and operational safeguards appropriate to the service. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. This policy may be updated as the platform evolves; the effective date above will be revised when material changes are published.
