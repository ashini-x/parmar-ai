The answer was only one part of the problem.

While building परमार्थAI around real exam material, we found that getting a model to respond was not enough. A learner needed to check the passage behind an answer and return to the original page. A teacher needed to know that their material stayed within the right workspace. And every new product was facing the same document-processing work.

That meant handling uploads, OCR, page mapping, indexing, permissions, retrieval and recovery again and again. We began building BRɅIN to make that foundation reusable — so an application could work with knowledge it was authorised to use, while remaining responsible for its own users and final answers.

THE MARKET

The opportunity is in the layer between knowledge and applications.

This is not an empty market. Google Gemini Notebook offers a source-grounded notebook experience. Glean connects workplace knowledge while respecting existing permissions. Azure AI Search gives developers retrieval and document-access building blocks.

Those products address real parts of the problem. Our thesis is narrower: teachers, academies and learning platforms may need knowledge they can organise around their own people and subjects, then make available to more than one purpose-built application.

One knowledge layer. Different learning experiences.

01 / SOURCE OWNER

Teacher or academy

A teacher or academy owns its study material: subjects, notes and study PDFs. The knowledge starts with the people who create or organise it.

Core returns selected passages with their source-page references. The connected application owns end-user authentication, prompts, model generation and conversation state.

WHERE WE DIFFER

We are not claiming to have invented citations or access control. We are bringing document operations, teacher-owned knowledge spaces, application permissions and page-aware retrieval together as one reusable foundation for specialist learning products.

THE STARTING POINT

Start with learning. Earn the right to expand.

Our first focus is India-based independent teachers and tutors working with dense, subject-specific material for exam preparation and classroom teaching. From there, the same model could support coaching centres and academies managing knowledge across teachers and subjects, then publishers and learning platforms serving multiple products. That is a growth path to validate, not a list of customers already using BRɅIN.
